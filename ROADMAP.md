REVE SafeMind — Project Roadmap

Organization: REVE Technologies & Innovation LTD
Current status: Concept and planning
Initial location: Abuja, FCT, Nigeria
Last updated: October 2026

1. Project Vision

To develop a privacy-conscious digital platform that improves access to emotional well-being education, online safety guidance, and trusted support services for adolescent girls and young women in Nigeria.

2. Development Phases

Phase 1 — Requirements and Research

Target duration: Weeks 1–2

- Define user needs and core product requirements.
- Consult mental health and child-protection professionals.
- Identify applicable Nigerian data-protection and safeguarding obligations.
- Research and begin verifying appropriate referral services.

Deliverable: Reviewed product requirements and safeguarding plan.

Phase 2 — No-Code Prototype

Target duration: Weeks 3–5

- Build a mobile-friendly welcome page and dashboard.
- Create an educational well-being library.
- Develop a digital safety and protection centre.
- Design an emotional check-in with privacy-conscious defaults.
- Create a trusted-support directory interface.

Deliverable: Initial navigable prototype.

Phase 3 — Review and Testing

Target duration: Weeks 6–7

- Conduct usability and accessibility reviews.
- Review content with qualified professionals.
- Test privacy settings, navigation, and safety information.
- Address identified usability and safeguarding issues.

Deliverable: Documented test findings and an improvement plan.

Phase 4 — Supervised Pilot Preparation

Target duration: Weeks 8–10

- Finalize pilot procedures and evaluation questions.
- Establish appropriate consent and safeguarding arrangements.
- Confirm referral partners and escalation information.
- Complete security and privacy reviews before collecting participant data.

Deliverable: Pilot-ready version, subject to required approvals.

Phase 5 — Pilot and Evaluation

Target duration: After readiness approval

- Conduct a small, supervised pilot in Abuja, FCT.
- Measure usability, accessibility, engagement, and user feedback.
- Document limitations, lessons, and recommendations.
- Improve the platform before considering wider deployment.

Deliverable: Pilot evaluation report and prioritized development plan.

3. Success Measures

- Users can navigate the main features successfully.
- Educational content passes expert review.
- Support-directory entries are verified and maintained.
- Privacy and safeguarding requirements are documented and tested.
- Usability feedback informs measurable improvements.

4. Current Limitations

REVE SafeMind has not yet been prototyped or tested. The timelines are preliminary estimates and depend on funding, technical capacity, professional partnerships, and required reviews.

5. Future Opportunities

Potential future developments include carefully evaluated AI-assisted resource navigation, additional local languages, improved accessibility, and partnerships with qualified support providers.

AI diagnosis, automated risk scoring, and automated emergency intervention are outside the initial scope.
