REVE SafeMind — Safety and Privacy Framework

Status: Proposed safeguards requiring expert review.

1. Guiding Principles

REVE SafeMind will prioritize user dignity, privacy, accessibility, informed participation, and age-appropriate safeguarding.

2. Data Minimization

- Allow access to educational resources without an account.
- Keep emotional check-ins on the user's device by default.
- Avoid collecting names, precise locations, abuse narratives, or other sensitive details unless strictly necessary and appropriately protected.
- Explain clearly what information is collected and why.
- Establish retention and deletion procedures before collecting participant data.

3. Protection of Minors

Before testing with anyone under 18, establish appropriate consent or assent procedures, safeguarding arrangements, and any required ethics or regulatory approvals. Consult qualified child-protection specialists and Nigerian legal and data-protection advisers.

4. Responsible AI

AI features are not part of the initial prototype. Any future AI must be evaluated for accuracy, bias, privacy, age appropriateness, and harmful outputs.

AI must not be presented as a therapist or emergency service. It must not independently diagnose users, assign automated crisis-risk scores, or make emergency decisions.

5. Trusted Support Directory

- Verify each listed organization or service.
- Record the verification date and review schedule.
- Explain service availability and limitations.
- Provide a process for reporting incorrect or outdated information.
- Avoid guaranteeing a provider's quality or availability without evidence.

6. Urgent Support

The platform must clearly state its limitations and provide verified information on appropriate support services. It must not imply that REVE SafeMind automatically alerts responders or dispatches emergency assistance.

7. Security Controls

Before a pilot, assess secure communications, administrator permissions, access controls, backups, retention, and incident-response procedures. Restrict content management to authorized personnel.

8. Governance

Assign responsibility for safeguarding, privacy, content review, and incident handling. Assess obligations under applicable Nigerian data-protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, with qualified advice.

9. Pilot Readiness

Do not begin a real-user pilot until the prototype, privacy notice, safeguarding procedures, referral information, consent arrangements, and relevant security and ethics reviews are ready.

10. Transparency

REVE SafeMind is currently at the concept stage. These safeguards describe planned requirements and are not a claim that a deployed system has already passed a security or safeguarding audit.
