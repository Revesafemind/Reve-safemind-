REVE SafeMind

Digital Well-being, Safety and Protection for Young Women

Developed by: REVE Technologies & Innovation LTD
Project status: Concept and planning stage
Initial target location: Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria
Target users: Adolescent girls aged 15–19 and young women aged 20–24

About the Project

REVE SafeMind is a proposed mobile-friendly digital platform designed to improve access to emotional well-being education, online safety information, and trusted support services for adolescent girls and young women in Nigeria.

The project aims to address challenges including emotional distress, cyberbullying, online harassment, digital exploitation, privacy risks, and limited access to reliable support resources.

Proposed Features

- Emotional Well-being: Guided emotional check-ins and evidence-informed educational resources.
- Digital Safety Centre: Practical guidance on cyberbullying, online harassment, exploitation, and privacy protection.
- Well-being Library: Accessible information on stress management and healthy coping strategies.
- Trusted Support Directory: Information about verified mental health and safeguarding services.
- Privacy and Protection: Minimal data collection and privacy-conscious design.
- Future AI Integration: Responsible AI-assisted resource navigation and relevant educational recommendations.

Technology Approach

The initial prototype is planned as a mobile-friendly web application using a no-code development platform. Future AI and data-science features will be considered after appropriate testing, professional review, and implementation of necessary safeguards.

Current Development Status

REVE SafeMind is currently at the concept and planning stage. A functional prototype has not yet been developed, and user testing has not commenced.

The next steps are to develop the prototype, review the educational content with qualified professionals, establish verified referral partnerships, and conduct supervised usability and safety testing.

Safeguarding and Privacy

The project prioritizes user privacy, age-appropriate safeguarding, accessibility, and responsible technology use. The initial version will not diagnose mental health conditions or replace professional care. Appropriate consent, security, data-protection, and ethical review procedures will be established before pilot testing, particularly for participants under 18.

Project Goals

1. Improve access to reliable well-being information.
2. Strengthen digital safety awareness and resilience.
3. Make trusted support resources easier to find.
4. Develop a privacy-conscious platform suitable for future evaluation and wider implementation.

Collaboration Opportunities

REVE SafeMind welcomes potential collaboration with mental health professionals, child-protection specialists, technology developers, researchers, community organizations, and funding partners.
